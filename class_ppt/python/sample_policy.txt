# Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how employees request and use paid leave. It applies to full-time employees unless a local employment rule provides a more generous benefit.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees become eligible for annual leave after completing 30 calendar days of employment. Contractors and interns follow the terms in their individual agreements.

## 3. Annual Leave
Eligible employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year. Carried-over leave must be used by March 31 or it expires.

Managers should consider business coverage before approving leave. Requests longer than 5 consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

## 4. Sick Leave
Employees may use sick leave when they are ill, recovering from treatment, or caring for an immediate family member. A medical certificate may be requested for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## 5. Parental Leave
Parental leave is administered according to local law and the employee's location. Employees should contact People Operations as early as practical so that eligibility, documentation, and dates can be confirmed.

## 6. Approval Process
Leave requests are submitted in the HR system. The employee's manager approves or rejects the request. People Operations can help resolve policy questions or conflicts with local requirements.
