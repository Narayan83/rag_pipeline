The incorrect interpretation of word “execution” of document levied at three percent instead of four percent had resulted in short levy of stamp duty of Rs. 689140/- as detailed  in Annexure-B.

In reply the Sub Registrar stated that matter would  be verified in detail and final compliance wiould be submitted to audit after consultation with the higher authority.
The expeditious steps would be taken for early action under intimation to audit.
