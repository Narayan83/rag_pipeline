ORDER: 
In the reference 2nd read above, Government have issued comprehensive orders for 
implementation of Revised Pay Scales-2022 , to the State Government employees. Further , 
among others, Government have specified at Para 11 of the reference 2nd read above , to 
constitute a Committee to examine the issue of Special Pay and the existing guidelines on 
Special Pays. 
2. Accordingly, in the reference 3rd read above , Government have constituted a 
Committee , to examine the issue of Special Pay and the existing guidelines on Special Pays 
and to give their recommendations to the Govenunent. After detailed examination of the 
matter , the Committee have opined to implement the recommendations of 11 th PRC, in respect 
of Special Pays. 
3. The eleventh Pay Revision Commission in its report on Special Pays, recommended for 
revision of rates on Special Pays. It is also recommended that no Special Pays be paid in 
addition of any new category to the list of employees eligible for grant of Special Pay. 
4. Government after careful examination of the matter , hereby order that Special Pays be 
paid at the revised rates to the holders of the posts as specified in the Annexure. 
5. These orders shall come into force from 01.07.2023 . 
6. Necessary amendments to the Andhra Pradesh Manual of Special Pays and Allowances 
shall be issued in due course . 
7. This order is available online and can be accessed at http://apegazette.cgg.gov.in .
