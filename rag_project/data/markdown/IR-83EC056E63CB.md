Part-I

(Introductory)

The Medical Superintendent, ESI Hospital, Amritsar, Punjab administers health insurance and medical benefits to workers covered under the Employees’ State Insurance Act include implementing ESI policies, managing healthcare facilities, ensuring timely access to medical services for eligible employees and their dependents and also overseeing and implementing various healthcare programs and policies in the district.

Contract Details:  The office of the Medical Superintendent, ESI Hospital, Amritsar, Punjab is situated at Majitha Road, Amritsar-143001 having email identification as esihospital55@gmail.com.

Reference Number: OBS-1447539

Para 1: Idle Machinery and equipment .

Punjab Financial Rules provides that purchase must be made in most economic manner and in accordance with definite requirement so that it does not prove unprofitable to the Government.

During scrutiny of records and information furnished by the O/o the MS, ESI Hospital, Amritsar it was noticed that a CBC Machine (Hematology Analyzer) valuing Rs.16.40 lakh was received from the Directorate in this office on dated 19/02/2020.  It was further revealed that the machine was lying idle since its receipt due to non availability of reagents for running the machine except for one week (8/11/2022 to 15/11/2022).

This resulted into blockade of government money in absence of reagents which was used in procurement of the machine.

On being pointed out, it was stated by the department that matter regarding reagent will be raised from the Headquarter office. Final compliance would be awaited.

Reference Number: OBS-1447941

Para 2:  Shortfall in conducting of meeting of Hospital Development Committee.

In order to improve overall functioning of ESI hospitals, the ESIC in its 143rd meeting approved constitution of Hospital Development Committees (HDC) for all State run ESI hospitals. Accordingly, Deputy Medical Commissioner, ESIC, New Delhi issued (July 2008) instructions for setting up of HDCs in the hospitals of State. The performance of hospital/attached dispensaries was to be reviewed by the HDC by holding its meeting at least once in every two months.

Scrutiny of records of ESI Hospital Mohali revealed that the Hospital Development Committee at ESI Hospital Mohali was constituted by the Government of Punjab, Department of Health and Family Welfare.  Moreover, against prescribed 30 meetings required to be held during the period 2019-20 to 2023-24, no meeting was held by HDC except one meeting held on 29/4/2024 during the year 2024-25.The above facts shows that HDC was not functioning as envisaged.

On being pointed out, it was stated by the department that the meeting could not be conducted due non availability of all the members at a time.  However, efforts would be made in future to conduct HDC meetings.  Final compliance would be audit in audit.

Reference Number: OBS-1454286

Para 3 : Shortage of staff.

Deployment of adequate manpower is essential for smooth functioning and providing proper maintenance of records etc. of any Government office.

During test check of records maintained in the O/o the MS, ESI- Hospital, Amritsar, it was noticed that against 207 sanctioned post in various cadres, only 103 posts (i.e., 49.76%) are filled, leaving 104 number of posts vacant (i.e., 50.24%) resulting into shortage of staff as on 06/2024.  It was further revealed that one sanctioned post of ACFA was sanctioned but the same was lying vacant sice long time which encouraged the poor maintenance of record.  In account branch, only six clerks were posted against 10 sanctioned posts due to which working of accounts branch was hampered.  In Drug Store, one post of Chief Pharmacy Officer was lying vacant against one sanctioned post and one post of Phamacy Officer was also lying vacant against five anctioned posts which impacted management and dispensing of medicines to the insured persons in the drug store.  In diagnostic and lab department, no ECG technician was posted against two sanctioned posts due to which ECG was being done by non technical persons.  Two posts of SMLT/MLT was also lying vacant against three

sanctioned posts.  
Audit observed that there was significant vacancy in various posts like sweeper, chowkidar, ward servant etc which needed to be filled on priority basis for smooth functioning, taking care of cleanliness and adequate sercurity and timely service delivery in the hospital.

Shortage of staff not only undermines the functioning of the Hospital but also maintenance of records, etc in proper condition.

On being pointed out in audit, the department replied that the matter regarding shortage of staff will be taken up with the higher authority for proper arrangement of staff. Therefore, final compliance would be awaited.

Para 4: Non disposal of unserviceable articles

Rule 15.3 of PFR Vol-1 provides that all head of offices/department should prepare list of unserviceable items for machinery, stores every year on 1st January and 1st July. The committee constituted for the purpose should record findings and ensure disposal of unserviceable articles so as to fetch revenue by its auction/sale.

During test check of records, it was noticed that 23 unserviceable articles having book value of Rs. 20,190/- were lying for disposal but no action had been taken by department for the auction of the same as the passage of time could deteriorate the condition of material which may cause the minimum proceeds from the sale of material as detailed below.

Reference Number: OBS-1456334

Para 5: Short availability of mandatory medicines

Employees State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), New Delhi decided (November 2015) that all the ESIS Hospitals and Dispensaries should strive towards achieving the desired norms and desired level of services but at the same time ESIC will monitor that minimum levels of services are mandatorily always available in these hospitals and dispensaries. One of minimum level of services include availability of 20 mandatory drugs for the ESI dispensaries and in addition to 20 mandatory drugs, 51 mandatory drugs (total 20+51=71) for the ESI hospitals.

During scrutiny of stock register as well as demand raised by the hospital and further information furnished by the ESI hospital, Amritsar, it was noticed that out of total 71 mandatory drugs, ESI hospital, Amritsar, had raised demands of only 29 drugs to the Directorate office leaving non raising of demands of 42 drugs. Further, out of 29 drugs demanded, only 24 drugs were available in the hospital which resulted in short availability of 47 (66%) mandatory drugs in the hospital. Short availability of drugs in the hospital not only violates the directions ibid but also deprival of intended benefits to the beneficiaries.

On being pointed out, Department stated that the audit observation has been noted for future compliance. Final compliance would be awaited in audit.

Reference Number: OBS-1456347

Para 6: Improper maintenance of Cash Book.

Rule 2.2(i) to 2.2(iv) of Punjab Financial Rules Vol I provides that every officer receiving money on behalf of government is required to maintain a cash book in form PFR I.  All the monetary transactions should be entered in cash book as soon as they occur and attested by the head of the office (DDO) as token of check. Cash book should be closed regularly and promptly checked. At the end of the month the head of the office should verify the cash book balance and record a signed and dated certificate to that effect.

During test check of cash book maintained in the O/o Medical Superintendent, ESI Hospital, Amritsar, following discrepancies were noticed in maintenance of cash book:

Cuttings were not attested,

Monetary transaction of the previous months was recorded in the next month

Transactions occurred were not attested by the head of the office.

Cash book was not closed regularly as receipts were not deposited timely, resultantly cash balance was lying pending at the end of the month.

Improper maintenance of cash book was against the financial rules ibid and in such circumstances the possibility of misappropriation of government funds could not be ruled out in audit.

On being pointed out in audit, Department stated that needful would be done and would be shown at the time of next audit. Final compliance would be awaited in audit.
