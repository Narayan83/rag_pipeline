Details
Non-formation of Stores Purchase Committee, Lower Purchase Committee and Negotiation Committee in APCPDCL and awarding contracts worth Rs. 37.61 Crore merely on nomination basis.

Detailed description

Government of Andhra Pradesh vide G.O. Ms. No. 41, Energy (Power-I) Department, dated 05-12-2019 had issued orders for bifurcation of Andhra Pradesh Southern Power Distribution Company Limited as Andhra Pradesh Southern Power Distribution Company Limited (APSPDCL) and Andhra Pradesh Central Power Distribution Corporation Limited (APCPDCL).  Subsequently, the Corporation filed declaration of commencement of business with Registrar of Companies on 20th January 2020.  However, the Corporation got distribution license on 01st April 2020.

Scrutiny of records it was observed that before bifurcation as APCPDCL, the APSPDCL has constituted Stores Purchase Committee, Lower Purchase Committee and Negotiation Committee for purchase of materials and awarding of contracts above Rs.100 lakhs and below Rs.100 lakhs.

However, after incorporation as APCPDCL, the DISCOM has not issued orders for constitution of the said Committees to date i.e. even after a lapse of more than 21 months.

Further, as per the G.O.MS.No.79 dated 25.08.2020 issued by Finance (HR.V-TFR) Department, reverse tendering procedure shall be mandatory in respect of procurement of Goods, Services and Works with a value of Rs.1.00 crore (Rupees one crore) and above.

On Scrutiny of the following purchase orders for procuring materials, it is noticed that without inviting tenders Contracts were awarded to following Contractors merely on Nomination Basis:

Company due to non formation of above committees, the tenders were not called for and also contrary to the above said Government Order, placing of purchase orders on nomination basis without inviting tenders is not in order. When the reasons for non-formation of Stores Purchase Committee, Lower Purchase Committee and Negotiation Committee in APCPDCL were called for, the Corporation promised compliance to the same.

Hence, proper reasons recorded for non-invitation of tenders may be stated to audit. The progress made in the formation of the Committees may be intimated to Audit.
